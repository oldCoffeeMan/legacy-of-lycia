# 🌍 World-Building Bible

## 1. Geography

- Southwest Anatolia; mountains, valleys, cedar forests, Mediterranean coast.
- Real topography underpins the game map (OpenStreetMap + DEM data).

---

## 2. Political Structure

- **Lycian League** – federal democracy with weighted votes.
- Persian Satrapy context – tribute without direct occupation.

---

## 3. Economy & Trade
| Resource | City Focus | Note |
|-----------|-------------|------|
| Olive Oil | Myra / Limyra | religious and economic |
| Cedar Wood | Tlos / Arycanda | shipbuilding |
| Wine | Tlos / Pinara | morale |
| Bronze & Iron | Olympos | weapons |
| Textiles | Xanthos | wealth |
| Pottery | Pinara | culture |

Coins minted per city with unique emblems; floating rates.

---

## 4. Society
- Matrilineal inheritance.  
- Citizens, artisans, women landholders, slaves (rare).  
- Education in temples; arts and sculpture valued.

---

## 5. Religion & Myth

**Deities:** Leto (fertility) • Apollo (light) • Artemis (nature) • Hephaestus (forge)
**Temples:** Letoon, Patara, Olympos
**Practices:** Oracles and dream rituals guide narratives.

---

## 6. Warfare & Piracy
- Light infantry & triremes.  
- Pirates as factions – Olympos, Phaselis, Kekova.  

## 7. Law & Governance
- “Code of Lycia”: restitution over punishment.  
- League council handles disputes and reforms.

---

## 8. Factions

**League Council** | **Pirate Brotherhood** | **Priestly Circle** | **Persian Envoys** | **Artisan Guild** | **Chroniclers**

---

## 9. Myths & Story Seeds
- Fire of Hephaestus (Olympos)  
- Tears of Leto (sacred spring)  
- Sleeping Oracle (awakens when League divided)  
- Lion Tomb of Xanthos (guarding souls)

---

## 10. Aesthetic Motifs

**Symbols:** Sunburst (light) • Wave (water) • Lion (strength) • Lotus (purity)
**Palette:** Warm amber stone + turquoise = signature look
